# Horizon

**A Manitoba map of supports and resources for Indigenous students navigating post-secondary education.**

[horizonmap.ca](https://horizonmap.ca/)

## About

Horizon is a web-based resource platform that connects Indigenous students in Manitoba with the supports they need to succeed in post-secondary education. By aggregating resources from post-secondary institutions, government agencies, industry partners, social services, and community organizations into a single searchable directory, Horizon makes it easier for students to find help across every aspect of their lives.

Built on the principle that **Student Success = Societal Success**, the platform recognizes that student achievement creates positive outcomes at the individual, family, community, and societal levels.

## Support Categories

Horizon organizes resources into 11 categories covering the full spectrum of student needs:

| Category | Description |
|---|---|
| Academic | Educational support services |
| Financial | Funding and financial aid resources |
| Housing | Accommodation assistance |
| Health | Medical and wellness resources |
| Career Pathways | Professional development guidance |
| Transportation | Travel and mobility assistance |
| Family | Familial relationship resources |
| Community | Local engagement opportunities |
| Advocacy | Rights and representation support |
| Emergency Services | Crisis intervention access |
| Urgent Services | Immediate crisis support |

## Foundation of Reconciliation

Horizon is grounded in the principles of UNDRIP, the Truth and Reconciliation Commission Calls to Action, Indigenous rights, and diversity, equity, and inclusion initiatives.

## Sources of Support

Resources are drawn from:

- Post-secondary institutions and preparatory programs
- Government agencies
- Industry sector and business councils
- Financial institutions and chambers of commerce
- Social service organizations
- Community outreach programs
- Philanthropic initiatives

## Contributing

Community members can submit, edit, and update resources directly through the platform at [horizonmap.ca](https://horizonmap.ca/).
